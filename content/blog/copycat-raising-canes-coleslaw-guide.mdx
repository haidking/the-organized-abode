## The Secret Ingredient: Celery Salt

While many homemade coleslaw recipes rely solely on mayo, vinegar, and sugar, Raising Cane's signature flavor relies on one crucial spice: **celery salt**.

Celery salt adds an aromatic savory backbone that cuts through the rich mayo and tangy apple cider vinegar, creating that addictive fast-food flavor profile.

---

## Tips for Best Flavor & Texture

- **Whisk Dressing First:** Always whisk the mayonnaise, sugar, vinegar, milk, celery salt, pepper, and salt in your mixing bowl *before* adding the cabbage. This ensures sugar dissolves completely and dressing coats evenly.
- **Chill for 2 Hours:** Letting the assembled slaw sit covered in the fridge for 2 hours allows the cabbage to soften slightly and absorb the dressing.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I substitute the apple cider vinegar?
White vinegar works as a 1:1 substitute, but apple cider vinegar provides a subtle fruity sweetness that matches Cane's original recipe best.

### How long does Cane's copycat coleslaw last?
Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 4 days. Give it a quick toss with a spoon before serving as dressing settles at the bottom over time.
