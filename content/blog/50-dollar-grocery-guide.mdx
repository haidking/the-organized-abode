Eating healthy on a strict budget often feels frustrating when specialty health food stores push expensive superfoods and organic luxury items.

However, when you focus on **nutrient-dense, high-yield base staples**, eating whole foods for **$50 per week ($2.40 per meal across 21 meals)** is not only possible — it is incredibly satisfying.

---

## The 4 Pillars of a $50 Grocery Budget

1. **Category 1: High-Yield Proteins ($18)** — Eggs (2 dozen), ground turkey (1 lb), canned tuna (4 cans), Greek yogurt (1 large tub), store rotisserie chicken (1 whole).
2. **Category 2: Nutrient-Dense Produce ($12)** — Bagged spinach, bananas, sweet potatoes, frozen broccoli, cucumber, apples.
3. **Category 3: Frugal Pantry Staples ($12)** — Rolled oats, jasmine rice, black beans, soy sauce, garlic, olive oil.
4. **Category 4: Flavor Extras ($8)** — Whole wheat bread, jarred salsa, hot sauce, seasonings.

---

## 3 Rules for Maximum Grocery Efficiency

- **Never Shop Without a Categorized List:** Straying into middle snack aisles adds $20–$30 of low-satiety impulse buys.
- **Embrace Frozen Produce:** Frozen broccoli and berries are flash-frozen at peak harvest, retaining full vitamin content at half the fresh produce cost.
- **Batch Cook Grains & Proteins on Sunday:** Pre-cooking rice and roasting sweet potatoes ensures meal assembly takes less than 3 minutes.
