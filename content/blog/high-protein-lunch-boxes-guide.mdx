Workday lunches are notorious for sabotaging healthy eating goals. Between busy schedules, overpriced salad bars, and mid-afternoon energy slumps caused by high-carb fast food, staying consistent with clean nutrition requires a game plan.

The solution? **High-Protein Lunch Box Prep.** 

By spending just 20 minutes on Sunday, you can assemble 6 distinct, macro-balanced lunch boxes containing 30g+ of high-quality protein each — ensuring you stay energized and satisfied through your busiest afternoons.

---

## Why Glass Containers Matter for Meal Prep

If you are serious about weekly meal prep, throw away flimsy plastic takeout containers and invest in a set of **glass meal prep containers with airtight locking lids**.

* **No Odor or Stain Transfer:** Glass won't absorb curry, tomato sauce, or garlic smells.
* **Safer Reheating:** Microwave glass directly without worrying about chemical leaching or warped plastic lids.
* **Better Moisture Preservation:** Airtight glass locking lids keep produce crisp and prevent cooked meats from drying out for up to 5 full days in the fridge.

---

## The 20-Minute Batch Cooking Strategy

Preparing 6 different lunches in 20 minutes sounds impossible until you master **parallel batch processing**:

1. **Sheet Pan Protein (15 Mins):** Season chicken cutlets, steak strips, and salmon fillets on one large divided sheet pan. Bake at 400°F for 14 minutes.
2. **Grain Base (15 Mins):** While proteins bake, cook a pot of quinoa or jasmine brown rice (or use pre-cooked pouch grains!).
3. **Vegetable Assembly (5 Mins):** Chop raw cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, and bell peppers while grains steam.
4. **Assembly Line (5 Mins):** Portion grains, veggies, and sliced proteins into 6 glass meal prep boxes.

### 5. Egg Salad Lettuce Wrap Box (30g Protein)
Hard-boiled eggs and whites mashed with high-protein Greek yogurt, wrapped in crisp romaine leaves alongside raw celery sticks and almonds.

### 6. Sirloin Steak & Sweet Potato Box (40g Protein)
Seared sirloin steak strips paired with roasted sweet potato cubes, steamed green beans, and flavorful green chimichurri sauce.

## Keeping Salads & Sauces Fresh All 5 Days

Nothing ruins meal prep faster than soggy veggies caused by pre-dressing your lunch boxes.

* **Separate Dressing Cups:** Store dressings, salsas, and tzatziki dips in small 2-oz stainless steel or silicone condiment cups inside your box.
* **Drying Raw Veggies:** Wash and dry cucumbers, tomatoes, and greens thoroughly with paper towels before packing so excess moisture doesn't create sogginess.
