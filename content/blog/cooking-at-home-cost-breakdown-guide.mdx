## How to Maximize Savings

1. **Buy Grains & Legumes in Bulk:** 5-lb bags of rice and dry beans lower per-serving carbohydrate costs to under $0.15.
2. **Batch Brew Coffee Concentrate:** Pitcher cold brewing yields a full week of premium iced coffee for less than $3.00 total.
3. **Master 2-Minute Sauces:** Making homemade mayo-based sauces saves hundreds annually compared to buying specialty jars or extra takeout sides.
