Salmon is one of the healthiest proteins you can eat, loaded with heart-healthy Omega-3 fatty acids and high-quality protein. However, pan-searing salmon filets often leads to uneven cooking or a smoky kitchen.

Enter **Air Fryer Salmon Bites**: bite-sized cubes of salmon tossed in savory seasonings and cooked in the air fryer in under 10 minutes.

---

## Why Salmon Bites Beat Whole Filets

Cutting salmon into bite-sized 1-inch cubes changes the game:

* **Faster Cooking:** Salmon bites cook in just 7 to 9 minutes!
* **More Surface Area for Crispy Crust:** Every single bite gets coated in delicious seasoning and caramelized edges.
* **Easy Portioning:** Perfect for building grain bowls, salads, or tacos.

---

## Ideal Air Fryer Temp & Timing

To prevent the salmon from drying out while ensuring a golden exterior:

* **Temperature:** **400°F (200°C)**. High heat cooks the salmon quickly before the interior loses moisture.
* **Time:** **7 to 9 minutes**, shaking the basket halfway through.

---

## The Honey Garlic Glaze Science

The key to irresistible salmon bites is adding a sweet and savory glaze during the last 2 minutes of cooking.

### Glaze Ingredients:
* 2 tbsp Soy Sauce (or Coconut Aminos)
* 1 tbsp Honey
* 1 tsp Sesame Oil
* 2 cloves Minced Garlic
* 1/2 tsp Grated Fresh Ginger

> **Pro Tip:** Don't toss the salmon in honey before air frying from the start! High sugar burns quickly at 400°F. Brush the glaze on during the final 2 minutes so it caramelizes into a glossy coating.

---

## Fresh vs. Frozen Salmon Tips

* **Fresh Salmon:** Skinless Atlantic or King salmon works best because of its fat content.
* **Frozen Salmon:** Thaw completely in the fridge overnight and pat thoroughly dry with paper towels. Removing excess surface moisture is essential for crispiness.

---

## Serving Suggestions

Build an impressive weeknight dinner bowl:

* **Teriyaki Salmon Bowl:** Serve over steamed jasmine rice with edamame, cucumber slices, and sriracha mayo drizzle.
* **Salmon Tacos:** Layer salmon bites inside warm corn tortillas with cabbage slaw and avocado lime crema.
