We have all been there: alarm goes off late, kids need lunch packed, or emails are already piling up. Cooking an elaborate breakfast is out of the question. But skipping breakfast or relying on sugary pastries sets you up for fatigue and cravings.

Here is how to make **real, nutritious breakfasts in 5 minutes or less** with zero cooking skills required.

---

## The Trap of Skipping Breakfast

Skipping breakfast often causes cortisol spikes and mid-morning hypoglycemia, leading to overeating at lunch. Eating even a quick 5-minute breakfast fuels your brain and keeps your metabolism hummed.

---

## The 3 Categories of 5-Minute Breakfasts

Simplify your morning routine by sticking to 3 quick formulas:

### 1. Toast Upgrades
* **Avocado & Everything Bagel Toast:** Sourdough, smashed avocado, hemp seeds, and everything seasoning.
* **Ricotta & Honey Fig Toast:** Whole grain bread, ricotta cheese, sliced fresh figs, and honey drizzle.

### 2. Grab-and-Go Jars
* **Chia Seed Pudding:** Chia seeds soaked overnight in almond milk, topped with berries.
* **Prep-Ahead Overnight Oats:** Oats, milk, Greek yogurt, and peanut butter.

### 3. High-Protein Yogurt Bowls
* **Greek Yogurt & Granola Bowl:** Greek yogurt topped with pumpkin seeds, sliced almonds, and berries.
* **Cottage Cheese & Peach Bowl:** Cottage cheese, canned peaches in juice, and cinnamon.
