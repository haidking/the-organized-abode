Popularized by metabolic researchers and fitness experts alike, the **"30g Morning Protein Habit"** recommends consuming at least 30 grams of high-quality protein within 30 to 60 minutes of waking up.

While traditional carbohydrate-heavy breakfasts (cereal, bagels, pastries, pancakes) trigger rapid glucose spikes followed by mid-morning crashes, a high-protein breakfast elicits completely different hormonal responses.

---

## 3 Physiological Shifts That Happen

### 1. Ghrelin Suppression & PYY Elevation
Protein intake directly suppresses **ghrelin** (the primary hunger hormone) while stimulating **peptide YY (PYY)** and **GLP-1** (satiety hormones). This extends full satiety for 4 to 5 hours.

### 2. Blood Glucose & Insulin Stabilization
By eliminating the morning glycemic spike, blood glucose remains flat and stable. This eliminates the 10:30 AM office sugar craving and prevents mid-day brain fog.

### 3. Thermic Effect of Food (TEF) Boost
Protein has a thermic effect of **20–30%**, meaning your body burns 20 to 30 calories out of every 100 protein calories consumed just to digest and metabolize it (compared to only 5–10% for carbs and 0–3% for fats).

---

## The 7-Day Experience Timeline

- **Day 1–2:** Satiety lasts until 1:00 PM without needing morning coffee refills or snacks.
- **Day 3–4:** Afternoon sugar and sweet cravings decrease noticeably.
- **Day 5–6:** Improved workout recovery and steady mental focus throughout work hours.
- **Day 7:** Natural hunger cues recalibrate, establishing effortless caloric self-regulation.
