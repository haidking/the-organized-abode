Colonel Sanders’ secret blend of 11 herbs and spices is arguably the most famous commercial recipe secret in culinary history. For decades, home cooks have attempted to replicate KFC’s signature savory coating, golden craggy ridges, and juicy tender interior.

Following the famous 2016 leak of the handwritten Sanders family herb blend notes, culinary enthusiasts finally confirmed the exact spice ratios. When combined with a buttermilk soak and a proper double-dredging method, you can make copycat KFC Original Recipe Chicken at home that surpasses the fast-food drive-thru!

---

## Decoding the Secret 11 Herbs and Spices Blend

The secret blend isn't complex, but the proportions are specific. Here is the exact breakdown mixed into 2 cups of all-purpose flour:

1. **Salt (⅔ tbsp)** – Essential flavor enhancer.
2. **Thyme (½ tbsp)** – Herbaceous back note.
3. **Basil (½ tbsp)** – Sweet herbal warmth.
4. **Oregano (⅓ tbsp)** – Pungent Mediterranean bite.
5. **Celery Salt (1 tbsp)** – Savory umami depth.
6. **Black Pepper (1 tbsp)** – Sharp heat.
7. **Dried Mustard (1 tbsp)** – Tangy zesty punch.
8. **Paprika (4 tbsp)** – Dominant golden color driver.
9. **Garlic Salt (2 tbsp)** – Deep garlic foundation.
10. **Ground Ginger (1 tbsp)** – Sweet aromatic warmth.
11. **White Pepper (3 tbsp) – The True Secret Driver!**

> **The Secret Key:** Notice the high ratio of **white pepper** (3 tablespoons!). White pepper provides KFC's distinct fermented earthy aroma and lingering tongue heat without turning the flour coating dark brown.

## Why Buttermilk Soaking Is Essential

Soaking raw chicken pieces in cultured buttermilk for at least 30 minutes (or overnight) serves two crucial functions:

* **Enzymatic Tenderization:** The natural lactic acid in buttermilk breaks down tough muscle fibers in chicken thighs and breasts, ensuring meat stays succulent and juicy during cooking.
* **Coating Binder:** The thick viscosity of buttermilk allows flour to stick securely to every crevice of the meat without sliding off.

---

## The Double-Dredge Technique for Maximum Crunch

To achieve KFC's signature craggy, thick crispy ridges without commercial pressure fryers:

1. **First Dredge:** Remove buttermilk-soaked chicken, letting excess drip off, and dredge thoroughly in the seasoned 11-spice flour mixture.
2. **Egg Wash Dip:** Submerge coated chicken briefly into an egg and milk bath.
3. **Second Dredge:** Dip chicken back into the spice flour mix. **Press flour firmly onto the chicken** so small crumbles and clusters adhere to the surface. Those flour crumbles turn into ultra-crunchy ridges when cooked!

## Air Fryer vs. Oven-Baked vs. Deep Frying

| Cooking Method | Temp & Time | Oil Needed | Crunch Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Air Fryer (Recommended)** | 380°F for 22-25 mins | Light cooking spray | 10/10 (Super Crispy, Low Fat) |
| **Oven Bake (Wire Rack)** | 420°F for 30-35 mins | Light oil spray | 8/10 (Golden & Clean) |
| **Traditional Deep Fry** | 350°F oil for 14-16 mins | Submerged in oil | 10/10 (Classic Fast-Food) |

Air frying chicken on a wire rack spray-coated with cooking oil produces near-identical crunch to deep frying with 80% fewer calories!

---

## Reheating Instructions for Leftover Fried Chicken

Never microwave leftover fried chicken—it turns soggy and rubbery!

To restore peak crunch, reheat cold chicken pieces in a **375°F air fryer for 4 to 5 minutes** (or in a 400°F oven on a wire rack for 8 minutes). The fat in the skin crisps back up instantly.
