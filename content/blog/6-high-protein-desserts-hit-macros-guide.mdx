Cravings don't disappear just because you're watching your macros or building muscle. The problem with traditional desserts is that they're loaded with refined sugars and saturated fats while offering almost zero satiety or protein.

The solution? **High-Protein Desserts** that turn staple fitness ingredients into rich, indulgent treats delivering **15g to 25g of protein per bowl**!

---

## The Ultimate High-Protein Dessert Base Ingredients

You don't need obscure supplements or chemistry kits to make delicious protein sweets. Keep these four staple ingredients in your kitchen:

1. **Low-Fat Cottage Cheese:** When blended in a food processor, cottage cheese completely loses its curds and transforms into a velvety, cheesecake-like cream base with zero sour taste.
2. **Plain Non-Fat Greek Yogurt:** Thick, tangy, and packed with casein protein that keeps you full overnight.
3. **Whey-Casein Protein Blend:** Casein absorbs liquid slowly, creating thick puddings, mousses, and batters rather than runny mixtures.
4. **Unsweetened Cocoa Powder & Peanut Butter Powder (PB2):** Rich chocolate and peanut butter flavor with a fraction of the fat and calories.

> **Pro Tip:** If using protein powder in microwave mug cakes or puddings, blend half whey and half casein. 100% whey protein turns rubbery when microwaved, while casein stays soft and cake-like!

---

## 3 Quick Dessert Ideas You Can Make in 5 Minutes

* **Chocolate Peanut Butter Cottage Cheese Mousse (24g Protein):** Blend ½ cup cottage cheese, 1 tbsp cocoa powder, 1 tbsp PB2, 1 tbsp maple syrup, and 1 scoop chocolate protein powder until silky smooth. Chill for 10 minutes!
* **Greek Yogurt Edible Cookie Dough (22g Protein):** Mix ½ cup Greek yogurt, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, 1 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp oat flour, and mini dark chocolate chips.
* **Microwave Protein Mug Cake (20g Protein):** Whisk 1 scoop chocolate casein protein powder, 1 tbsp cocoa powder, ½ tsp baking powder, 1 egg white, and 3 tbsp almond milk in a mug. Microwave for 45 seconds!

---

## How to Prevent Protein Desserts From Tasting Chalky

* **Add Healthy Fats:** A single tablespoon of natural peanut butter, almond butter, or heavy cream rounds out the mouthfeel and masks any artificial sweetener aftertaste.
* **Don't Over-Microwave:** Protein bakes dry out in seconds. Stop microwaving mug cakes while the center is still slightly gooey.
