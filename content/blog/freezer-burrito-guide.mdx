A hot, high-protein breakfast is the best way to jumpstart energy and satiety for the day. However, cooking eggs, turkey, and beans every morning takes time that most busy weekdays don't afford.

**Freezer breakfast burritos** solve this problem completely. By investing 30 minutes on Sunday, you can assemble 8 burritos packed with **38g of protein each** that reheat to perfection in just 90 seconds.

---

## 3 Secrets to Preventing Soggy Burritos

### 1. Cool Ingredients Completely
Never assemble burritos with steaming hot eggs or turkey meat! Heat produces steam condensation inside wrapped tortillas, resulting in mushy, soggy burritos upon thawing. Let all hot fillings reach room temperature before rolling.

### 2. Layer Moisture Away from the Tortilla
Place dry elements (like shredded cheese or spinach) directly against the flour tortilla base. Place moist ingredients (salsa, scrambled eggs, beans) in the center core.

### 3. The Damp Paper Towel Reheat Method
When reheating from frozen, remove aluminum foil, wrap the burrito in a single damp paper towel, and microwave on high for 90 to 120 seconds. The damp towel steams the tortilla skin so it stays soft and pliable rather than tough or rubbery.
