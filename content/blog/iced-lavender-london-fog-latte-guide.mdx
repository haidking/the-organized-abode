A traditional London Fog latte is a cozy hot beverage made from Earl Grey black tea, vanilla syrup, and steamed milk. But when warm weather rolls around (or when you need an afternoon boost), turning this classic into an **Iced Lavender London Fog Latte** is pure perfection.

The combination of bergamot-infused black tea, subtle floral lavender, warm vanilla extract, and cold creamy milk creates a sophisticated coffeehouse drink that tastes like a luxury treat.

---

## The Secret to a Strong, Non-Watered-Down Tea Base

The biggest mistake people make when preparing iced tea lattes is brewing a normal cup of hot tea and pouring it over ice. The ice instantly melts, diluting the delicate bergamot notes of Earl Grey into a weak, watery mess.

**The Solution:** Brew a **concentrated tea base**!

* Steep **2 Earl Grey tea bags** in just **¼ cup (2 oz) of hot water** for 5 full minutes.
* This produces a rich, dark tea concentrate that retains its bold flavor even after cold milk and ice cubes are added!

---

## Culinary Lavender Syrup Options

You can purchase pre-made culinary lavender syrup online or at specialty grocery stores, or easily make your own 2-ingredient syrup at home:

* **Homemade Lavender Syrup:** Simmer ½ cup water, ½ cup sugar (or honey), and 1 tablespoon of dried organic culinary lavender buds for 5 minutes. Strain and cool!
* **Vanilla Balance:** Vanilla extract is essential in a London Fog — it softens the floral lavender notes so the drink tastes sweet and balanced rather than soapy.

> **Pro Tip:** Pair Earl Grey with oat milk (especially barista blend oat milk). Oat milk has a natural creaminess and subtle sweetness that complements black tea better than dairy or almond milk.

---

## How to Layer Your Iced London Fog

1. **Step 1:** Stir lavender syrup and vanilla extract directly into the hot concentrated Earl Grey tea.
2. **Step 2:** Fill a tall glass with fresh ice cubes.
3. **Step 3:** Pour the sweetened Earl Grey tea concentrate over the ice.
4. **Step 4:** Top with cold oat milk (or frothed milk for a cloudy foam layer) and garnish with a pinch of lavender buds!
