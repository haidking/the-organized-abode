As autumn approaches, morning routines benefit from cozy, warm seasonal spices. But baking pumpkin loaves every morning isn't practical.

These **5 Pumpkin Spice Overnight Oats variations** bring real canned pumpkin purée, cinnamon, nutmeg, and maple sweetness into healthy, high-fiber breakfast jars prepped on Sunday evening.

---

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

- **10-Minute Meal Prep:** Line up 5 mason jars, fill, seal, and refrigerate for the whole work week.
- **Real Pumpkin Nutrition:** Canned pumpkin purée provides rich Vitamin A, fiber, and potassium with zero added junk.
- **5 Distinct Flavors:** Enjoy a different autumn twist every single morning.

---

## 5 Fall Flavor Variations

1. **Classic Pumpkin Pie:** Pure pumpkin purée, maple syrup, chia seeds, and warm pumpkin pie spice.
2. **Pumpkin Pecan Crunch:** Classic base topped with crunchy toasted pecans and brown sugar.
3. **Maple Pumpkin Chai:** Classic base infused with aromatic chai spice blend and pure maple syrup.
4. **Dark Chocolate Chip Pumpkin:** Classic base folded with dark chocolate chips for dessert-for-breakfast vibes.
5. **Vanilla Protein Pumpkin:** Classic base whisked with 1 scoop vanilla whey protein powder (25g protein).

---

## Golden Rules for Pumpkin Overnight Oats

- **Use Pure Pumpkin Purée:** Check ingredient labels to ensure you purchase 100% pure canned pumpkin purée, *not* pumpkin pie mix which contains heavy syrups.
- **Chia Seeds are Mandatory:** Chia seeds absorb liquid overnight, creating a thick, pumpkin-pie custard texture.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I eat pumpkin overnight oats warm?
Yes! While delicious cold straight from the fridge, you can microwave your jar (without the metal lid) for 45–60 seconds for a cozy warm porridge.

### How long do pumpkin overnight oats last?
Stored in sealed mason jars in the fridge, overnight oats remain fresh for up to 5 days.
