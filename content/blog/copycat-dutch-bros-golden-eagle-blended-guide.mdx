If you're a fan of Dutch Bros Coffee, chances are you've fallen in love with the **Golden Eagle Blended Freeze**. It's easily one of their most popular signature drinks — creamy, rich, sweet, and bursting with buttery caramel and smooth vanilla notes.

However, buying a daily blended drink at the drive-thru can easily cost $7+ per cup. The good news? You can blend your own Golden Eagle at home in under 5 minutes for less than $1.50 per serving!

---

## The Secret: Dutch Bros "Kick Me Mix"

The secret behind Dutch Bros' addictively smooth texture is their proprietary **Kick Me Mix**, which is simply **half-and-half** (equal parts whole milk and heavy cream).

Most traditional iced coffees use skim or 2% milk, which separates or gets watery when blended with crushed ice. Using half-and-half provides enough milkfat to bind with the ice, creating a velvety, milkshake-like texture without needing artificial thickeners or powder bases.

---

## Key Ingredients Needed

1. **Espresso or Strong Cold Brew Concentrate:** 2 shots of fresh espresso work best. If using cold brew, ensure it's a strong concentrate so the coffee flavor cuts through the rich cream.
2. **Vanilla Syrup:** Classic vanilla coffee syrup (such as Torani or Monin).
3. **Caramel Sauce:** Rich caramel ice cream topping or coffee caramel sauce for blending and rim decoration.
4. **Half-and-Half:** For that authentic Kick Me Mix richness.
5. **Ice:** 1½ cups of clean ice cubes.

> **Pro Tip:** Let your hot espresso shots cool for 2-3 minutes before adding them to the blender with ice. Pouring piping hot espresso directly onto ice causes instant melting, making your blended drink watery!

---

## How to Get the Perfect Slushy Blend

* **Blend in Stages:** High-speed blending for 30-45 seconds creates a smooth texture without leftover ice chunks.
* **Caramel Drizzle Art:** Swirl caramel sauce around the inside of your glass before pouring the blended mixture. Not only does it look visually stunning, but you also get sweet caramel ribbons with every straw sip.
* **Top it Off:** Finish with a swirl of whipped cream and an extra drizzle of caramel sauce over the top!
