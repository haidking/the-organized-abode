When hot weather hits or late-night dessert cravings strike, nothing beats a refreshing bowl of fruit sorbet. But commercial ice cream shop sorbets are often packed with corn syrups, artificial flavorings, and unnecessary preservatives.

This **3-Ingredient Mango Sorbet** comes together in just 5 minutes using a simple food processor or blender. It's 100% dairy-free, naturally sweet, and impossibly smooth — without needing an expensive churn machine!

---

## The Science Behind Machine-Free Sorbet

Traditional sorbet machines work by continuously churning liquid fruit puree while freezing it, breaking up ice crystals so the sorbet stays smooth.

To skip the machine, you simply **reverse the process**:

1. Start with pre-frozen fruit chunks (frozen mango).
2. Blend at high speed in a food processor or high-powered blender.
3. The heavy blades pulverize the frozen fruit fibers into microscopic frozen crystals, instantly producing a soft-serve sorbet texture!

---

## The 3 Essential Ingredients

* **Frozen Mango Chunks (4 cups):** Buying pre-frozen bagged mango chunks from the grocery store saves prep time and ensures the fruit was frozen at peak sweetness.
* **Fresh Lime Juice (2 tbsp):** Fresh lime juice is the secret flavor enhancer — it cuts through the rich tropical sweetness of the mango and brightens every spoon.
* **Honey or Agave Nectar (3 tbsp):** Natural liquid sweetener prevents the sorbet from freezing into a rock-hard block while adding a subtle floral finish. (Use agave or maple syrup for a vegan version!)

> **Pro Tip:** If your blender struggles to grab the frozen chunks initially, add just 1-2 tablespoons of cold water or coconut water to help the blades turn. Avoid adding too much liquid, or you'll turn your sorbet into a smoothie!

---

## Soft-Serve vs. Firm Scoop Texture

* **Serve Immediately:** Enjoy straight out of the blender for a luxurious soft-serve texture.
* **Firm Scoopable Balls:** Transfer the blended sorbet into a metal loaf pan or container and freeze for 45-60 minutes. Use a hot ice cream scoop dipped in warm water to scoop perfect restaurant-style sorbet balls!

---

## Fun Flavor Variations

* **Mango Chili Lime (Tajín):** Sprinkle a dash of Tajín seasoning over the top for an authentic Mexican mangonada twist!
* **Tropical Mango Coconut:** Replace water with 2 tbsp of canned full-fat coconut milk for an ultra-creamy mango gelato texture.
