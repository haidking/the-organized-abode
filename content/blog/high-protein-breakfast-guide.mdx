If you find yourself reaching for a snack by 10:00 AM after eating a bagel or sugary cereal for breakfast, your morning meal is likely lacking one crucial macro: **protein**.

Eating a protein-rich breakfast stabilizes blood sugar, suppresses ghrelin (the hunger hormone), and provides sustained energy throughout the morning.

---

## Why Protein at Breakfast Matters

Nutritionists recommend aiming for **25 to 40 grams of protein** during your first meal of the day. A high-protein breakfast:

1. **Increases Satiety:** Protein takes longer to digest than refined carbohydrates, keeping you full for hours.
2. **Supports Muscle Maintenance:** Distributing protein intake evenly across all meals optimizes muscle protein synthesis.
3. **Curbs Mid-Morning Cravings:** Prevents energy spikes and crashes associated with high-sugar breakfast foods.

---

## 10 Quick High-Protein Breakfast Ideas

Here are 10 delicious options ranging from 25g to 42g of protein per serving:

1. **Cottage Cheese & Berry Bowl (30g protein):** 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese topped with fresh berries, chia seeds, and honey.
2. **Protein Overnight Oats (32g protein):** Rolled oats, Greek yogurt, chia seeds, and 1 scoop chocolate whey protein.
3. **Turkey Sausage & Egg Wrap (35g protein):** Whole wheat tortilla filled with scrambled egg whites, turkey sausage, and spinach.
4. **Greek Yogurt Power Parfait (28g protein):** 1 cup plain non-fat Greek yogurt layered with high-protein granola and sliced almonds.
5. **Smoked Salmon & Egg White Toast (30g protein):** Toasted sourdough topped with avocado, 3 scrambled egg whites, and 3 oz smoked salmon.
6. **High-Protein Waffles (25g protein):** Made with oat flour, egg whites, and vanilla protein powder.
7. **Scrambled Tofu & Black Bean Burrito (26g protein):** Seasoned tofu scramble with black beans, salsa, and nutritional yeast.
8. **Egg & Bacon Bites (28g protein per 3 bites):** Baked egg muffins made with turkey bacon, cheddar, and spinach.
9. **Peanut Butter Protein Smoothie (34g protein):** Frozen banana, chocolate protein powder, peanut butter, and soy milk.
10. **Sourdough Egg-in-a-Hole with Beef Patties (40g protein):** 2 eggs cooked inside sourdough slices served with lean ground beef patties.

---

## Meal Prep & Calorie Target Tips

* **Batch Cook:** Bake egg bites or prepare overnight oats on Sunday for quick grab-and-go access all week.
* **Keep Ingredients Stocked:** Always keep frozen berries, Greek yogurt, eggs, and cottage cheese in your fridge.
