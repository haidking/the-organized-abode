When hunger strikes in the afternoon, reaching for potato chips or sugary granola bars causes rapid blood sugar spikes followed by severe energy crashes.

These **9 lazy, high-protein snack combinations** deliver **15g to 25g of protein with zero cooking required** and take under 2 minutes to assemble.

---

## Why High-Protein Snacking Matters

- **Sustained Energy:** Protein slows digestion, keeping you satisfied for 3–4 hours without mid-afternoon cravings.
- **Muscle Preservation:** Spacing protein throughout the day boosts muscle protein synthesis better than consuming all your protein in one giant dinner.
- **No-Cook Convenience:** Every single snack on this list can be eaten straight out of the fridge or pantry.

---

## 9 Snack Ideas at a Glance

1. **Cottage Cheese & Pineapple:** 20g protein — creamy cottage cheese paired with sweet tropical pineapple.
2. **Greek Yogurt & Peanut Butter Swirl:** 21g protein — 0% Greek yogurt folded with creamy peanut butter and honey.
3. **Turkey & Cheddar Roll-Ups:** 22g protein — roast turkey wrapped around sharp cheddar sticks.
4. **Steamed Edamame & Sea Salt:** 18g protein — warm shelled edamame dusted with coarse salt.
5. **Tuna & Avocado Crackers:** 26g protein — albacore tuna and mashed avocado on whole grain crackers.
6. **Hard-Boiled Eggs & Everything Seasoning:** 13g protein — pre-boiled eggs with everything bagel spice.
7. **Roasted Chickpeas & Almonds:** 15g protein — crunchy roasted chickpeas paired with raw almonds.
8. **Beef Jerky & String Cheese:** 18g protein — peppered beef jerky and mozzarella string cheese.
9. **1-Minute Protein Mug Cake:** 28g protein — chocolate whey, egg, and milk microwaved for 45 seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which high-protein snack is best before a workout?
Greek yogurt with honey or a banana with peanut butter provides fast-digesting carbs and protein ideal for fueling physical exercise.

### How do I meal prep these snacks for the work week?
Portion nuts, chickpeas, and jerky into small reusable containers on Sunday. Pre-boil a dozen eggs and keep Greek yogurt cups stocked in your fridge.
