Outback Steakhouse’s signature Bloomin’ Onion is famous worldwide, but true fans know that the real star of the show is the **creamy, smoky, zesty dipping sauce** served alongside it.

This secret sauce blends rich mayonnaise, horseradish punch, ketchup sweetness, and smoky spices. Best of all, you can make a restaurant-identical bowl in **5 minutes** using simple pantry ingredients.

---

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

- **5-Minute Prep:** Just measure, whisk, and chill. No cooking or blenders required.
- **Pantry Staples:** Made with ingredients you likely already have in your fridge and spice rack.
- **Versatile Dip:** Exceptional with onion rings, French fries, chicken tenders, roast beef, or as a burger spread.

---

## The Secret Ingredient Ratios

The key to recreating Outback's exact sauce flavor profile lies in balancing heat, acidity, and creaminess:

1. **The Creamy Base:** Mayonnaise provides velvety body and rich mouthfeel.
2. **The Zesty Kick:** Prepared horseradish (not creamed horseradish) delivers that signature subtle nose-tingling heat.
3. **The Sweet & Smoky Balance:** Ketchup adds natural sweetness and acidity, while smoked paprika and cayenne pepper layer in earthy warmth.
4. **Herbal Under-Notes:** Garlic powder, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper tie all the elements together.

---

## Pro Tips for Maximum Flavor

- **Chill Before Serving:** While you can eat this sauce immediately, chilling it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes allows the dried herbs and horseradish to fully hydrate and infuse into the mayonnaise.
- **Adjusting the Heat:** For a milder sauce, reduce cayenne pepper to ¼ tsp. For extra kick, bump it up to ¾ tsp or add a dash of hot sauce.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does homemade Bloomin' Onion sauce last?
Stored in an airtight jar in the refrigerator, this sauce stays fresh and delicious for up to 2 weeks.

### What is the difference between prepared horseradish and horseradish sauce?
Prepared horseradish consists of grated horseradish root preserved in vinegar and salt. Horseradish sauce or cream contains added mayonnaise or sour cream. For this recipe, stick to prepared horseradish for authentic bold flavor.
