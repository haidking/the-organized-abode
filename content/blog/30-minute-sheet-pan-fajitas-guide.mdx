Fajitas are a timeless family favorite, but standing over a smoking skillet tossing peppers and meat in multiple batches can turn your stove into a splattered mess.

Enter **Sheet Pan Fajitas**: everything — juicy chicken or steak, sliced bell peppers, and sweet yellow onions — roasts together on a single parchment-lined sheet pan at high heat. You get all the sizzling flavor and blistered edges of skillet fajitas with virtually zero mess to clean up afterward!

---

## The Secret to Crispy, Charred Sheet Pan Fajitas

The key to getting restaurant-quality fajitas in the oven comes down to **temperature and pan spacing**:

1. **High Oven Heat (425°F / 220°C):** Roasting at high heat cooks sliced chicken or steak quickly while scorching the edges of the peppers and onions before they lose their crunch.
2. **Don't Crowd the Pan:** Use a large rimmed half-sheet baking pan (18x13 inches). If ingredients are piled three layers high, they will steam instead of roast.
3. **Parchment Paper Lining:** Lining your sheet pan with unbleached parchment paper prevents sticking and makes cleanup as simple as throwing away the paper!

> **Pro Tip:** Slice your onions and bell peppers slightly thicker (around ¼-inch) than your meat cutlets. This ensures the veggies hold their bite and caramelize at the exact same pace as the chicken or steak.

---

## Homemade Fajita Seasoning vs. Store-Bought Packets

Store-bought fajita seasoning packets often contain anti-caking additives, excess sodium, and artificial thickeners. Making your own blend takes 60 seconds with basic pantry spices:

* **1 tbsp Chili Powder**
* **1 tsp Ground Cumin**
* **1 tsp Garlic Powder**
* **1 tsp Onion Powder**
* **½ tsp Smoked Paprika** (for authentic wood-fired smokiness!)
* **½ tsp Dried Oregano**
* **Salt & Cracked Black Pepper**

---

## Chicken vs. Steak: Slicing Tips

* **Chicken Breast or Thighs:** Slice against the grain into long thin strips.
* **Flank Steak or Skirt Steak:** Always slice thinly **across the grain** at a 45-degree angle. Cutting across muscle fibers guarantees tender, melt-in-your-mouth steak strips rather than chewy bites.

## Perfect Serving Ideas

Serve your sheet pan fajitas straight from the pan with:

* Warm flour or corn tortillas
* Fresh lime wedges & chopped cilantro
* Creamy homemade guacamole or sliced avocado
* Fresh pico de gallo & sour cream
