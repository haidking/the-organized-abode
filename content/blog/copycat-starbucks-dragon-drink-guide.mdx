Starbucks' famous **Dragon Drink** has taken summer beverage menus by storm, combining sweet mango flavor, creamy coconut milk, and striking neon-pink dragonfruit pieces.

However, paying $6 every morning adds up fast. With this simple 3-ingredient copycat hack, you can recreate the exact coffee-shop favorite in **2 minutes for under $1.50 per cup**.

---

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

- **Huge Money Saver:** Costs $1.50 per serving compared to $6.00 at Starbucks.
- **2-Minute Prep:** Just pour, shake with ice, and serve.
- **Vivid Magenta Hue:** The natural dragonfruit fruit pieces create an irresistible, Instagram-worthy pink gradient.

---

## The 3 Essential Ingredients

1. **Mango & White Grape Juice Base:** Provides sweet tropical fruit flavor and natural sweetness matching Starbucks' refresher base.
2. **Creamy Coconut Milk:** Adds smooth velvety body that turns clear fruit juice into a dreamy pastel refresher.
3. **Freeze-Dried Dragonfruit (Pitaya) Pieces:** Rehydrates in the cold liquid, releasing natural electric-pink pigment and subtle floral sweetness.

---

## Tips for Coffee-Shop Perfection

- **Shake Don't Just Stir:** Shaking the juice, coconut milk, and ice together in a shaker or jar creates a frothy top layer that mimics Starbucks' baristas.
- **Carton vs. Canned Coconut Milk:** Use unsweetened coconut milk from a carton for a lighter daily drink, or canned coconut milk for an ultra-creamy dessert treat.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I buy freeze-dried dragonfruit?
Freeze-dried dragonfruit (pitaya) slices or powder can be found in the dried fruit aisle of major grocery stores (Trader Joe's, Target, Whole Foods) or ordered online.

### Does the Dragon Drink contain caffeine?
The Starbucks store version contains green coffee extract for mild caffeine. At home, you can keep it caffeine-free for kids, or add ¼ tsp of green coffee powder if you want an energy boost!
