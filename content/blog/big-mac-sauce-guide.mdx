For decades, home cooks believed that McDonald's secret Big Mac sauce contained French dressing or Thousand Island dressing. However, McDonald's executive chefs eventually revealed the truth: there is zero ketchup or French dressing in real Big Mac sauce!

Making authentic copycat Big Mac sauce at home takes less than 5 minutes and relies on 5 core pantry ingredients.

---

## The 5 Core Ingredients

Here is the authentic copycat formula:

1. **Mayonnaise Base:** Real mayonnaise (like Hellmann's or Duke's) provides rich creaminess.
2. **Sweet Pickle Relish:** Delivers signature sweetness and texture.
3. **Yellow Mustard:** Adds bright acidity and yellow color.
4. **White Wine Vinegar:** Provides sharp tanginess.
5. **Garlic & Onion Powder:** Infuses savory background flavor.

---

## Why Relish Creates the Signature Tang

The secret behind the recognizable flavor is sweet pickle relish combined with vinegar and mustard. The vinegar cuts through the fatty mayonnaise, while sweet relish balances the savory spices.

> **Pro Tip:** Stir in 1/2 tsp of sweet paprika for authentic warm orange hue and subtle smokiness!

---

## Mayo Base vs. Miracle Whip Debate

Should you use Miracle Whip? **No.** Miracle Whip contains added sugar and spices that alter the flavor profile. Stick to classic mayonnaise for authentic restaurant taste.

## 10 Ways to Use Big Mac Sauce Beyond Burgers

Don't limit this sauce to burgers! Try it on:

1. Dip for French fries or tater tots
2. Salad dressing for a "Burger in a Bowl"
3. Spread for turkey wraps or grilled cheese
4. Dipping sauce for chicken tenders
5. Topping for baked potatoes
6. Sauce for fish tacos
7. Spread for breakfast egg sandwiches
8. Drizzle over roasted vegetables
9. Dressing for potato salad
10. Secret sauce for smash burger sliders
