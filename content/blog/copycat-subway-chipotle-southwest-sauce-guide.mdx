Ask anyone what makes Subway's Steak & Cheese, Chipotle Chicken, or Turkey Sub taste so memorable, and they'll likely mention one thing: **Subway's Chipotle Southwest Sauce**.

That rich, creamy, smoky, and slightly spicy sauce elevates even a simple sandwich into something crave-worthy. Luckily, recreating this restaurant sauce at home requires zero cooking and takes just 2 minutes of whisking!

---

## What Ingredients Are in Subway's Chipotle Southwest Sauce?

While the commercial restaurant version uses vegetable oil emulsions and preservatives, you can achieve a much fresher, richer flavor at home with simple kitchen ingredients:

1. **Mayonnaise & Sour Cream Base:** Using a 2:1 ratio of mayonnaise to sour cream gives you the ideal creamy richness with a subtle tangy lift. (Swap sour cream for plain Greek yogurt for a protein boost!)
2. **Chipotle Peppers in Adobo Sauce:** This is the non-negotiable flavor driver! Canned chipotle peppers (smoked jalapeños) packed in adobo sauce supply that authentic wood-smoked heat.
3. **Lime Juice:** Freshly squeezed lime juice brightens the cream and balances the smoky heat.
4. **Southwest Spice Blend:** Garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cumin, and salt.

> **Pro Tip:** Mince your chipotle peppers finely into a smooth paste (or pulse in a small food processor) before whisking into the mayo base so the sauce stays silky smooth.

---

## Heat Level Adjustments

One of the best benefits of making your own sauces at home is controlling the heat level to suit your taste:

* **Mild & Zesty:** Use 1 tablespoon of adobo sauce from the can and only ½ minced chipotle pepper.
* **Classic Subway Style:** Use 1 full chipotle pepper minced plus 1 tablespoon of adobo sauce.
* **Extra Spicy:** Add 2 minced chipotle peppers plus a dash of cayenne pepper!

---

## Delicious Ways to Use Chipotle Southwest Sauce

Don't limit this sauce to subs! It's an unbelievably versatile condiment:

* **Sandwiches & Wraps:** Spread on turkey bacon wraps, grilled chicken paninis, or smash burgers.
* **Dipping Sauce:** Serve with air fryer fries, chicken tenders, onion rings, or quesadillas.
* **Taco & Bowl Drizzle:** Drizzle over fish tacos, grain bowls, or breakfast burritos.

---

## Storage & Shelf Life

Store your homemade Chipotle Southwest Sauce in an airtight glass jar or squeeze bottle in the refrigerator. Because it contains mayonnaise and sour cream, it stays fresh and flavorful for up to **2 weeks**!
