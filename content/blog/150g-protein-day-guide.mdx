Hitting **150 grams of protein per day** can sound daunting, especially if you're trying to do it without relying entirely on endless dry chicken breasts or four protein shakes a day.

Whether your goal is muscle gain, fat loss, or improved recovery, the secret to reaching high protein targets sustainably lies in **protein distribution** throughout the day and pairing high-protein staples with nutrient-dense, whole foods.

---

## Why Protein Distribution Matters

Your body synthesizes muscle protein most effectively when protein intake is spread across **4 to 5 distinct meals or snacks** throughout the day, rather than consumed in one massive evening meal. Aiming for **30g to 40g of protein per meal** ensures optimal muscle protein synthesis (MPS) without causing bloating or digestion sluggishness.

### The Macro Blueprint (150g Protein Target)

- **Breakfast (30–35g):** Greek yogurt power bowl with whey protein or egg white scramble.
- **Morning Snack (25g):** Protein smoothie or cottage cheese bowl.
- **Lunch (40g):** Grilled chicken breast with jasmine rice and steamed green vegetables.
- **Afternoon Snack (20–25g):** Hard-boiled eggs or high-protein Greek yogurt dip.
- **Dinner (40g):** Lean sirloin steak, salmon fillet, or turkey bowls with roasted sweet potato.

---

## Top Real-Food Protein Powerhouses

To hit 150g effortlessly, keep these high-yield whole foods stocked in your fridge:

1. **0% Plain Greek Yogurt:** 15–20g protein per cup.
2. **Low-Fat Cottage Cheese:** 24–28g protein per cup.
3. **Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast:** 31g protein per 100g.
4. **Lean Ground Turkey (93/7 or 99/1):** 22–26g protein per serving.
5. **Egg Whites:** 26g protein per 1 cup liquid whites.
6. **Albacore Tuna:** 30g protein per 5 oz canned.
