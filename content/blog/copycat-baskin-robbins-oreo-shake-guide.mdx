Baskin-Robbins' **Oreo Ice Cream Shake** is legendary for its ultra-thick, spoonable texture and rich chocolate cookie specks in every single sip.

Making a milkshake at home often results in a runny, watered-down mess. With this copycat guide, you will master the exact **ice-cream-to-liquid ratio** to blend restaurant-quality shakes in **3 minutes**.

---

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

- **3 Ingredients:** Premium vanilla ice cream, Oreo cookies, and whole milk.
- **Blender Only:** No ice cream maker required — ready in under 180 seconds.
- **Ultra-Thick Consistency:** Perfectly balanced so it holds a straw upright.

---

## The Secret to Ultra-Thick Milkshakes

The most common milkshake mistake is adding too much milk. Here is how ice cream parlors get that signature scoopable thickness:

1. **Use Heavy Ice Cream Ratio:** Maintain a ratio of 3 cups of ice cream to just ½ cup of milk.
2. **Soften Ice Cream Slightly:** Let vanilla ice cream sit on the counter for 5 minutes before blending so it scoops easily without needing extra liquid.
3. **Pulse Don't Over-Blend:** Add Oreo cookies at the end and pulse on medium speed for 20 seconds. This leaves crunchy chocolate cookie bits throughout without turning the shake gray.

## Flavor Variations to Try

- **Peanut Butter Oreo Shake:** Add 2 tablespoons of creamy peanut butter to the blender.
- **Mint Chocolate Oreo Shake:** Use mint chocolate chip ice cream instead of vanilla.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use skim milk or oat milk?
Whole milk yields the creamy traditional taste, but oat milk or almond milk works great as a dairy-free alternative!

### Why did my milkshake turn gray?
Over-blending pulverizes the black Oreo cookies into fine dust, discoloring the vanilla ice cream. Pulse briefly so cookie bits remain distinct.
