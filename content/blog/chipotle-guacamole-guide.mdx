Chipotle's guacamole is famous for good reason: it is fresh, chunky, perfectly seasoned, and made in small batches throughout the day. And yes, everyone knows it costs extra!

The good news? Chipotle has publicly shared their exact guacamole recipe, proving that you only need **5 simple fresh ingredients** (plus salt) to make it at home.

---

## Chipotle's Official 5 Ingredients

1. **Hass Avocados:** Rich, creamy dark skin avocados.
2. **Fresh Lime Juice:** Provides bright acidity and prevents browning.
3. **Red Onion:** Finely diced for subtle sharp crunch.
4. **Jalapeño Pepper:** Seeded and finely diced for gentle heat.
5. **Fresh Cilantro:** Chopped leaves for herbal brightness.
6. *(Plus Kosher Salt to taste).*

---

## The Avocado Ripeness Palm Test

Great guacamole starts with perfect avocados. Avoid hard green avocados or bruised mushy ones.

* **Palm Test:** Gently squeeze the avocado in the palm of your hand. It should yield to gentle pressure without feeling soft or sunken.
* **Stem Test:** Flick off the small stem cap at the top. If it is green underneath, the avocado is perfect! If brown, it is overripe inside.

---

## Lime vs. Lemon & Cilantro Substitutions

* **Lime Only:** Authentic Chipotle guac uses 100% fresh lime juice. Lemon juice is too sweet.
* **Cilantro Haters:** If you have the gene that makes cilantro taste like soap, substitute with finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley or green onion tops.

---

## How to Keep Guacamole from Browning

Avocados brown when polyphenol oxidase enzymes react with oxygen (oxidation).

### Prevention Techniques:
1. **Lime Juice Layer:** Drizzle extra lime juice over top before storing.
2. **Flush Plastic Wrap:** Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole, eliminating all air pockets.
3. **Airtight Container:** Store in the coldest part of your fridge for up to 48 hours.
