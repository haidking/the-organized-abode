When weather turns cold, nothing hits the spot quite like Olive Garden's endless soup, salad, and breadsticks. Their **Chicken Gnocchi Soup** — packed with pillowy potato gnocchi, shredded chicken, spinach, and carrots in a rich cream broth — is the ultimate comfort food.

Making this copycat version at home takes just 30 minutes and yields a soup even richer than the restaurant version.

---

## The Secret to Velvety Cream Broth

Many copycat recipes rely on heavy flour roux bases that can turn paste-like.

### The Better Method:
Combine **chicken broth, half-and-half, and heavy cream** simmered with mirepoix (celery, onions, carrots) and garlic. As the potato gnocchi cook directly in the broth, they release natural starches that naturally thicken the soup into a velvety finish!

---

## Store-Bought vs. Homemade Gnocchi

Save time on weeknights by using **shelf-stable vacuum-packed potato gnocchi** found in the pasta aisle. They cook in just 3 to 4 minutes directly in the simmering soup broth.

---

## Rotisserie Chicken Shortcut

Skip cooking raw chicken from scratch! Use shredded meat from a store-bought **rotisserie chicken**. It adds instant savory flavor and cuts prep time in half.

---

## Reheating Tips

Because the soup contains dairy, reheat leftover soup gently over medium-low heat on the stove, stirring frequently. Avoid boiling leftovers so the cream does not separate.
