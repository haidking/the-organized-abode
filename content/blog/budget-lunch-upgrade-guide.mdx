With restaurant lunch prices climbing past $15 to $20 per bowl, bringing lunch from home has never been more relevant. However, sad desk salads and bland turkey sandwiches quickly lead to takeout temptation.

The solution? **Restaurant-style bowl assembly using high-flavor pantry sauces and smart texturing.**

---

## 4 Rules for a $3 Gourmet Lunch Bowl

### 1. Acid & Umami Drizzle
Commercial bowls taste incredible because of balanced acidity and umami. Keep soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and sriracha mayo on hand. A quick drizzle of sesame oil instantly elevates basic canned tuna or chicken.

### 2. Warm Grain Base + Cool Toppings
The contrast between warm jasmine rice and cool, crisp toppings (like cucumbers, radishes, and creamy avocado) creates a distinct restaurant eating experience.

### 3. Textural Crunch
Always finish your bowl with a crunch element: toasted sesame seeds, crispy fried onions, sliced green onions, or crushed seaweed (nori) snacks.

### 4. High-Yield Canned Seafood
Canned albacore or skipjack tuna packed in water or olive oil costs under $1.50 per can, providing 30g of clean protein for a fraction of raw sushi-grade fish costs.
