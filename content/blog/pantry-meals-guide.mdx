When the fridge looks bare at the end of the week, ordering food delivery is often the default choice. But if you keep just **5 fundamental pantry staples** in stock, you possess the building blocks for over **30 versatile, comforting meals**.

---

## The 5 Core Building Blocks

1. **Large Fresh Eggs:** High-quality protein, natural emulsifier, and richness.
2. **Jasmine or Long-Grain White Rice:** Versatile carbohydrate foundation.
3. **Extra Virgin Olive Oil:** Flavorful cooking fat and sauce base.
4. **Fresh Garlic Cloves:** Aromatic flavor enhancer.
5. **Canned Crushed or Whole Tomatoes:** Rich acidic and savory sauce base.

---

## Top Flavor Variations You Can Create

- **Shakshuka:** Poached eggs gently simmering in garlic-spiced tomato sauce.
- **Crispy Garlic Fried Rice:** Cold rice tossed in fragrant garlic olive oil topped with a crispy fried egg.
- **Classic 10-Minute Tomato Pasta Sauce:** Sautéed garlic in olive oil simmered with crushed San Marzano tomatoes.
- **Baked Eggs in Tomato Ramekins:** Eggs cracked into seasoned tomato sauce baked until golden.
- **Garlic Tomato Confit Scramble:** Creamy soft scrambled eggs folded with sweet olive oil simmered tomatoes.
