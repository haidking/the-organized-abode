The grocery store rotisserie chicken is arguably the single greatest budget food invention of all time. Typically priced between $6 and $8, a single rotisserie chicken yields enough cooked meat and bone stock to create **5 distinct, delicious dinners** for the week.

---

## Strategic Deboning & Storage

To stretch your rotisserie chicken effectively, breakdown the chicken as soon as you get home from the store while it is still warm:

1. **Breast Meat (White Meat):** Shred into large bites — best for quesadillas, wraps, and salads.
2. **Thigh & Leg Meat (Dark Meat):** Dice small — retains moisture best for high-heat fried rice, tacos, and soups.
3. **Carcass & Bones:** Never throw the carcass away! Cover with water in a stockpot and simmer with onion and garlic for 45 minutes to produce 4 cups of rich, gelatinous homemade chicken stock worth $4.00 on its own.
