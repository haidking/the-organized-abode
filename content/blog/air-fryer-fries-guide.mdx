We all love crispy French fries, but traditional deep frying leaves your kitchen smelling like oil and adds unnecessary calories. Air frying delivers that sought-after crunch using 80% less oil — if you know the proper potato prep techniques.

Learn the exact science behind making **Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Fries** that stay crispy for hours.

---

## Soaking vs. Not Soaking Potatoes

The #1 secret to crispy homemade fries is **soaking cut potatoes in cold water for 30 minutes**.

### Why Soaking Works:
Potatoes are loaded with surface starch. If left on the potato, this starch sticky caramelizes quickly during cooking, turning fries brown and soggy before the inside cooks through. Soaking draws out excess starch, allowing the exterior to crisp up properly.

---

## Basket vs. Tray Arrangement

* **Single Layer:** Never overcrowd your air fryer basket! Steam gets trapped when fries are piled high, resulting in limp fries.
* **Cook in Batches:** Cook fries in a single layer with space for air circulation.

---

## Garlic Butter Timing: Add AFTER Air Frying

Do not coat raw potatoes in minced garlic or parmesan cheese before air frying! Minced garlic burns at 400°F, turning bitter, while cheese melts onto the bottom basket.

### The Correct Method:
1. Air fry potatoes seasoned only with oil, salt, and cornstarch at 380°F for 15 mins, then 400°F for 5 mins.
2. Immediately transfer hot fries to a large bowl.
3. Toss with melted garlic butter, freshly grated Parmesan, and chopped fresh parsley.
