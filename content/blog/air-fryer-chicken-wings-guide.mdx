Whether hosting a game day party or craving a weekend treat, crispy chicken wings are always a crowd favorite. But deep frying leaves oil splatters everywhere, while oven baking often results in soggy skin.

Air frying chicken wings delivers deep-fried level crunch with zero oil bath!

---

## The Baking Powder Chemistry Secret

How do you get ultra-shatteringly crispy skin in an air fryer? **Aluminum-free baking powder**.

Baking powder is alkaline. When tossed with raw chicken skin, it raises the pH level, breaking down peptide bonds. This causes moisture to evaporate faster and skin to blister into microscopic bubbles during air frying.

> **Important:** Use **aluminum-free** baking powder (not baking soda!), or wings will taste metallic. Use 1 tablespoon baking powder per 2 lbs wings.

---

## Pat Dry Technique

Before tossing in baking powder, pat wings completely dry with paper towels until no moisture remains.

## 6 Incredible Homemade Sauces

1. **Classic Buffalo:** Frank's RedHot + melted butter + Worcestershire.
2. **Honey Garlic:** Soy sauce + honey + minced garlic + sesame oil.
3. **Lemon Pepper Butter:** Melted butter + lemon zest + cracked black pepper.
4. **Garlic Parmesan Ranch:** Melted butter + dry ranch mix + grated parmesan + garlic powder.
5. **Smoky BBQ:** Favorite BBQ sauce + apple cider vinegar + liquid smoke.
6. **Korean Gochujang:** Gochujang paste + honey + soy sauce + garlic.
