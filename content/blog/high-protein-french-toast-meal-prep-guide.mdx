Traditional French toast is delicious, but standard egg-and-milk batter offers low protein and leaves you hungry by mid-morning.

By blending **cottage cheese, whole eggs, egg whites, and cinnamon** into a rich custard batter, you can turn thick sourdough French toast into a **35g high-protein meal prep powerhouse** that reheats in a toaster in 60 seconds.

---

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

- **35g Protein per Serving:** Keeps you full and focused for hours without needing protein powders.
- **Zero Cottage Cheese Texture:** Blending the batter turns cottage cheese into a smooth, creamy vanilla-cinnamon custard — even cottage cheese skeptics love it!
- **Toaster-Friendly Meal Prep:** Cook a big batch on Sunday, store in the fridge, and pop slices into the toaster all week for crispy edges.

---

## Why Cottage Cheese Works Magic in Custard Batter

Cottage cheese is packed with **slow-digesting casein protein** and natural creaminess. 

When thoroughly blended with eggs, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla, the curds completely disappear. As the sourdough dips and bakes, the batter creates a fluffy soufflé-like interior with crisp golden edges.

---

## 3 Rules for Meal Prep Success

1. **Use Thick Sourdough or Brioche:** Sturdy, thick-cut sourdough bread holds up to heavy dipping without tearing or turning soggy.
2. **Cool on a Wire Rack:** After cooking on the griddle, let French toast cool on a wire rack so steam escapes from both sides before storing.
3. **Reheat in the Toaster:** Avoid microwaving meal prepped French toast. Reheating in a toaster or air fryer at 180°C (350°F) for 2 minutes restores that freshly cooked crunch.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I freeze cooked French toast?
Yes! Stack cooled French toast slices with parchment paper between them in a freezer bag. Freeze for up to 2 months. Reheat directly from frozen in a toaster on medium heat.

### Will it taste like cheese?
Not at all. Combined with cinnamon, vanilla, and maple syrup, the cottage cheese tastes like a rich, sweet egg custard.
