Instant ramen is the ultimate budget staple, but relying solely on the sodium-heavy powder packet leaves noodles swimming in flat, salty broth.

By applying classic Japanese ramen shop finishing techniques, you can transform inexpensive instant noodles into a creamy, deeply comforting bowl of gourmet ramen in under 10 minutes total.

---

## The 5 Instant Upgrades

1. **Butter & Soy Sauce Broth Emulsion:** Stirring 1 tablespoon of unsalted butter and 1 tablespoon of soy sauce into hot broth emulsifies fat into the liquid, mimicking rich tonkotsu or miso ramen broth body.
2. **6.5-Minute Jammy Soft-Boiled Egg:** A creamy, soft yolk soaked in seasoned ramen broth adds luxury to every bite.
3. **Chili Oil or Chili Crisp:** Delivers smoky heat and aromatic sesame aroma across the top layer of broth.
4. **Fresh Green Onions:** Adds crucial sharpness and fresh crunch to cut through rich broth fat.
5. **Nori Seaweed & Sesame Seeds:** Provides authentic umami aroma and texture.

---

## Sodium Reduction Secret

> **Pro Tip:** Use only **half of the included seasoning packet**. The addition of soy sauce, butter, chili oil, and fresh green onions builds superior flavor while reducing overall sodium by over 40%!
