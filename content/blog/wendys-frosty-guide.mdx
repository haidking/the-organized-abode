Is a Wendy's Frosty a milkshake, soft-serve ice cream, or something entirely unique? Wendy's founder Dave Thomas deliberately designed the Frosty to be eaten with a spoon because it was too thick to drink through a straw.

You don't need a bulky ice cream maker to recreate this iconic treat. You only need **3 ingredients** and a blender!

---

## The 3-Ingredient Secret

1. **Chocolate Milk:** Provides authentic chocolate dairy flavor.
2. **Sweetened Condensed Milk:** Delivers rich sweetness and smooth texture.
3. **Cool Whip (Whipped Topping):** Introduces air bubbles that replicate soft-serve machine aeration.

---

## Why Dipping Fries in a Frosty Works

Dipping hot, salty French fries into a cold, sweet Frosty is a legendary flavor pairing rooted in food science:

* **Salty + Sweet Contrast:** Triggers dual flavor receptors on the tongue.
* **Hot + Cold Temperature Difference:** Creates sensory contrast that enhances enjoyment.

---

## Chocolate vs. Vanilla Variations

Swap chocolate milk for whole milk and add 1 tsp pure vanilla extract to make a Copycat Vanilla Frosty!
