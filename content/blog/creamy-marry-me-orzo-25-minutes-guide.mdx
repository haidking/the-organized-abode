The viral "Marry Me" flavor profile — built on sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, rich cream, fresh herbs, and parmesan — gained its legendary name because it's supposedly so delicious that anyone you serve it to will want to propose on the spot.

While classic Marry Me Chicken is a staple, swapping chicken for tender, rice-shaped **orzo pasta** takes this dish to a whole new level of cozy one-pan comfort. Best of all, everything cooks together in a single pan in just 25 minutes!

---

## Why You'll Love Cooking Orzo One-Pan Style

Unlike traditional long pasta noodles that need boiling in a separate pot of water, **orzo absorbs liquid like arborio rice**.

1. **Starchy Texture:** Cooking orzo directly in broth and cream releases its natural starches into the pan, creating a thick, risotto-like creaminess without needing cornstarch or roux.
2. **Flavor Infusion:** Because the orzo simmers inside the chicken broth and sun-dried tomato oil, every single grain absorbs deep savory flavor from the inside out.
3. **One Pan to Wash:** Less cleanup means more time enjoying your evening.

> **Pro Tip:** Always toast your uncooked orzo in the hot sun-dried tomato oil for 2 minutes before pouring in the liquid. Toasting enhances the nutty aroma of the pasta and keeps it from getting mushy.

---

## Key Ingredients for Maximum Flavor

* **Sun-Dried Tomatoes in Oil:** Use tomatoes packed in oil rather than dry-packed. Don't discard the oil in the jar! Use 2-3 tablespoons of that vibrant reddish oil to sauté your garlic — it carries incredible concentrated flavor.
* **Freshly Grated Parmesan:** Block-grated Parmigiano-Reggiano melts smoothly into the warm cream sauce.
* **Fresh Baby Spinach & Basil:** Stirred in right at the end, spinach wilts in seconds and adds a pop of color and freshness to balance the rich sauce.

---

## Easy Add-Ins for Extra Protein

Want to make this a hearty protein-packed main course? 

* **Seared Chicken Breasts:** Sear seasoned chicken breast cutlets first, set them aside, cook the orzo in the same pan, then nestle the sliced chicken back on top before serving.
* **Garlic Butter Shrimp:** Toss in raw peeled shrimp during the last 3-4 minutes of simmering the orzo.
* **Crispy Chickpeas:** Stir in a can of drained chickpeas for a high-fiber vegetarian option.
